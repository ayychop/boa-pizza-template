Scope: the whole demo (Forno Rosso is sample branding in a template for independent pizza shops) ordering surface (hero, menu, item modal, cart, tracker, oven, find us). Mode: Persuade on the first viewport, Operate from the menu down.
Audience and job: hungry people in High Wycombe, often evening and late, on a phone, wanting to pick a pizza and know when it lands. Action: start an order, add to order, check out, watch it come.
Constraints: keep all app logic, menu items, prices and facts. Brief pins Anton display, Manrope body, #0A0A0A ground, electric red accent, cream text, red-tinted glass on cart and modals, glowing red primary action, thick red tracker bar.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the backlit menu board above a late-night slice counter, loud enough to read from the door. It refuses the fine-dining arrangement (italic serif, quiet cards, prices whispered) and the chain arrangement (deal tiles, coupon clutter).

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, one electric red, cream type. Anton set huge and uppercase for names, headlines and every price. Board panels: dark grained surface, thick 2px frame, red light on hover. A red ticker band runs the shop's facts like a sign over the door. Glass only on what floats, tinted red.

STORY: See fire-hot pizza and the exact time it would reach you tonight. Pick from a board where the price is as loud as the name. Watch your ticket move through the oven in an app-grade tracker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed fire-lit pizza photo. Left, a two-line Anton headline at about 13vw desktop. Under it one sentence, then a huge glowing red block "Start your order" with the live line "At your door by HH:MM" beside it. Red ticker band pinned to the bottom edge.

Signature interaction: the live door-time in the hero, computed from the clock and the chosen mode, echoed by the tracker ETA.
